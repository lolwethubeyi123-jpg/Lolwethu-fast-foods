# Lolwethu's Fast Food Kitchen

Website for Lolwethu's Fast Food Kitchen.

## About the Website

Lolwethu's Fast Food Kitchen is a fast-food website created to provide customers with information about the business, its menu, and contact details.

The website focuses on affordable and tasty fast food such as:

- Kotas
- Gatsbys
- Wings & Chips
- Russian & Chips
- Burgers
- Chips and Extras

## Website Pages

The website contains the following pages:

- **Home** - Introduction to Lolwethu's Fast Food Kitchen and featured food.
- **About Us** - Information about the business, its goal and why customers can choose us.
- **Menu** - Displays the available meals, prices and food images.
- **Enquiry** - Allows customers to submit an enquiry using a form.
- **Contact Us** - Provides the business location, phone number, email address, opening hours and a map.
