Our Story

Lolwethu's Fast Food Kitchen is a local fast food business based in Gqeberha. The business provides affordable, hot, and delicious meals for customers who enjoy high-quality fast food on the go.

We sell different types of freshly prepared meals including kotas, gatsbys, wings and chips, Russian and chips, and burgers.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide tasty food at reasonable prices and to make sure that our customers thoroughly enjoy their meals every single time they visit.

Why Choose Us?

  • Highly affordable food options
  • Different delicious meals to choose from
  • Convenient, central location
  • Friendly and fast service
  • Freshly prepared food every day
